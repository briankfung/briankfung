# Brian K. Fung, PharmD, MPH

**Clinician-Engineer · Founder · Clinical AI, Informatics, Interoperability & Public Policy**

I'm a residency-trained informatics pharmacist who designs, builds and ships clinical AI products. I founded Brian K Fung Media, LLC in 2025. Before that I was a Staff Clinical Informatics Data Architect at Verily and a Clinical Informatics Pharmacist at Mayo Clinic, where I modernized clinical systems across 80 hospitals and clinics. As a graduate intern at the Office of the National Coordinator for Health IT, I contributed to the electronic prescribing sections of the 21st Century Cures Act Final Rule.

## Products

- **[haau3](https://haau3.com)**: FHIR-native, AI-powered care management that helps caregivers focus on the human side of care. Generally available and listed in the CMS Medicare App Library.
- **[haau3 platform](https://platform.haau3.com)**: the APIs behind haau3, including a scheduling slot aggregator built on HL7 SMART Scheduling Links and a FHIR terminology service.
- **[Unscripted Rx](https://www.unscripted-rx.com)**: pharmacy residency and workforce discovery with 30,000+ connected entities.
- **[Ovant](https://www.ovant.org)**: a job search tool that learns your career once, then writes a resume for each role from what you have actually done.
- **[OpenClinTech](https://openclintech.com)**: courses and a live bootcamp that train clinician-engineers in HL7 FHIR, clinical informatics and AI in healthcare.
- **[TECC](https://tecc.dev)**: a cardio-kidney-metabolic care sandbox using FHIR, CDS Hooks and terminology services, aligned with CMS ACCESS and FDA TEMPO.

## Open source and standards

- **[HL7 SMART Scheduling Links](https://build.fhir.org/ig/HL7/smart-scheduling-links/en/)**: lead author of the FHIR implementation guide.
- **[@briankfung/fhir-types](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@briankfung/fhir-types)**: hand-rolled HL7 FHIR R4 TypeScript types, including US Core Patient.
- **[public-fhir-server](https://github.com/briankfung/public-fhir-server)**: FHIR R4 resource operations on Postgres, [live for testing](https://public-fhir-server.vercel.app).
- **[go-fhir-server](https://github.com/openclintech/go-fhir-server)**: a reference FHIR server in Go, built for learning.
- **[haau3 FHIR IG](https://github.com/haau3/fhir-ig)** and **[patient scheduling IG](https://briankfung.github.io/patient-scheduling/)**: implementation guides written in FHIR Shorthand.
